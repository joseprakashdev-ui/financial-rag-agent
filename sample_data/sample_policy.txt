COMMERCIAL CREDIT UNDERWRITING AND RISK MANAGEMENT POLICY
Document Ref: POL-CR-2026-v4.1 | Classification: Internal BFSI Regulatory Standard

Section 1: Executive Scope & Governance
This policy establishes mandatory underwriting benchmarks, debt covenant thresholds, and collateral
governance protocols for all commercial lending, syndicated credit facilities, and asset-backed real estate exposures.

Section 2: Maximum Loan-to-Value (LTV) Limits
2.1 Stabilized Commercial Real Estate (CRE): The standard maximum allowable LTV is 75.0%.
2.2 Construction & Value-Add Properties: Maximum allowable LTV shall not exceed 65.0% of appraised as-completed value.
2.3 Raw Land Acquisition: Maximum allowable LTV is capped at 50.0% with zero subordinate liens permitted.
2.4 Exception Protocol: Any facility exceeding 75.0% LTV requires unanimous Tier-2 Credit Risk Committee approval
    and additional liquid collateral equivalent to 120% of the loan excess amount.

Section 3: Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) Covenants
3.1 Base Threshold: Borrowers must maintain a minimum DSCR of 1.25x across all trailing twelve months (TTM) calculations.
3.2 High-Volatility Sectors: Hospitality, retail, and special-use commercial assets require a minimum DSCR of 1.40x.
3.3 Covenant Cure Period: If DSCR drops below 1.15x for two consecutive quarters, the borrower has 30 business days
    to deposit cash reserves sufficient to restore the 1.25x threshold or face immediate technical default covenants.

Section 4: Collateral Valuation & Verification Guidelines
4.1 Independent Appraisal: All real property securing loans >$1,000,000 must undergo independent appraisal certified by
    a MAI (Member Appraisal Institute) appraiser within 90 days prior to credit committee sanctioning.
4.2 Environmental Site Assessments (ESA): Phase I ESA is mandatory on all commercial parcels. Any identified
    Recognized Environmental Conditions (RECs) require Phase II testing and minimum 150% cleanup escrow reserves.
4.3 UCC Title Filings: First-priority perfected liens must be recorded on all operational fixtures and equipment.
